Key Patterns Across All Cases:
1. Design Phase: 3-4 weeks for initial concept and specification
2. Prototype Development: 2-3 weeks for sample production
3. Testing and Validation: 2 weeks for quality assurance
4. Mass Production: 5-6 weeks for full order fulfillment
5. Quality Control: 1 week for final inspections
6. Total Timeline: 12-15 weeks from concept to delivery

Common Success Factors:
- Clear specification of requirements upfront
- Regular communication throughout the process
- Early testing and validation to catch issues
- Defined MOQ requirements (typically 50 pcs per design)
- Defined lead times (30-45 days after PO confirmation)
- Quality control at every production stage
