## Race-Ready from the First Stroke

The RHEO Race Series is the elite-tier inflatable for competitive racers, speed enthusiasts and experienced paddlers who refuse to compromise. It bridges inflatable convenience and podium-topping performance with one uncompromising focus: maximum velocity, agility and streamlined efficiency.

### Core Design

- **Carbon Hybrid Construction**: A carbon hybrid matrix delivers unprecedented rigidity, eliminating flex entirely for a rock-solid, ultra-stiff platform
- **Aggressive Glide Rocker**: A performance-tuned rocker slices through water with minimal resistance, reducing drag and maximizing forward momentum with every stroke

### Performance

- **Hydrodynamic High-Speed Profile**: Shaped for smaller paddlers or anyone chasing explosive bursts of speed, with a silhouette that holds tight lines at peak velocity
- **Sharp Buoy Turns**: The refined racing geometry keeps the board agile, allowing precise turns and rapid responses in competitive environments

### Application Scenarios

- **Competitive SUP Racing & Sprints**: Official race circuits, technical sprints and time trials where every second counts
- **High-Speed Fitness Training**: Long, aggressive cardio sessions on open water
- **Challenging & Tight Waterways**: Narrow channels, tight pockets and technical courses
