### Miami Bound

Vatrad will exhibit at the 2026 Miami International Boat Show, February 11-15 at the Miami Marine Stadium Park and Basin. This marks Vatrad's debut at North America's largest boat show.

The Vatrad booth (B-312) will feature the full product lineup with hands-on demonstrations:

- **SUP Explorer 11** —All-around beginner inflatable SUP
- **SUP Tour 12** —Touring inflatable SUP with displacement hull
- **SUP Pro 14** —Performance touring SUP for advanced paddlers
- **SUP Yoga 10** —Wide stable SUP for yoga and fitness
- **Life Vest Pro** —Automatic-inflation life vest

On-water demos will be available by appointment, allowing attendees to test Vatrad SUPs on the Venetian Basin. Vatrad executives will also be available for B2B meetings with potential dealers and fleet operators.

Contact SUPsfactory to schedule a meeting or book an on-water demo.
