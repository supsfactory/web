Beginner SUP training is Afarer's primary use case. The stable, wide-body design of boards like the SUP Explorer 11' allows first-time paddlers to stand up within minutes, and the 32" width gives the generous beam that compensates for an unsteady stance during the first sessions.

## Why wide boards win for training

Stability is the single most important factor when a shop, club or school invests in a training fleet. A board that is too narrow or too stiff under foot will frustrate students before they develop balance. The all-around shape used across the entry class keeps the volume high and the rail forgiving, so learners can focus on paddle stroke rather than staying upright.

Inflatable construction adds a second layer of safety. Soft rails mean a fall — and there will be falls — produces contact with a padded surface rather than a rigid hard rail, which is why training programs consistently prefer inflatables over rigid boards.

## Typical training environments

Training fleets appear in three settings: calm lakes and sheltered bays, swimming pools, and enclosed lagoons. All three demand the same board behaviour — a wide, stable platform at 32 inches, predictable glide, and good flotation. A stable platform paired with a leash and a CE-certified life vest creates a safe learning environment, and the 11-foot size strikes the balance between stability and manoeuvrability for a mixed-ability group.

## Fleet planning for schools

For a training program buying its first fleet, we recommend starting with 8–10 identical all-around boards plus a matched set of paddles and life vests. Identical boards simplify storage, sizing and maintenance, and they let instructors rotate paddlers without recalibration. As skills progress, the same boards still serve at introductory level, while your touring-friendly position — look at the SUP Cheetah Surge or SUP Ocean Voyager for distance paddlers — covers the path to open water.

The SUP Explorer 11' is the recommended first-fleet board for this reason: one model covers the complete progression from first stand-up to an intermediate touring paddle.
