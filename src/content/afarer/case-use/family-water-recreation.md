Family water recreation is a core use case for afarer's larger inflatables. The Kayak Tandem seats two and the AirDeck 360 carries up to five people, making them ideal for family outings on lakes, calm rivers and sheltered coastal waters.

## The family boat problem

A family day on the water has different requirements than a solo session: two or more paddlers of very different weights, children whose balance is unpredictable, and gear — towels, water, picnic — that needs to come along. A single-person SUP fits none of those needs. What families actually buy is a stable, spacious inflatable that is easy to transport and store when the day is over.

## Tandem and multi-seat options

The Kayak Tandem pairs two paddlers in a stable, forgiving hull and is the recommended choice for couples and parents paddling with older children. For larger groups, the AirDeck 360 carries up to five people and doubles as a floating platform — anchor it, swim from it, and climb back aboard via the integrated handles. Both are drop-stitch inflatables, so they pack into a carry bag that fits a car boot or a locker at the lake club.

## Safety on family trips

All family recreation requires proper safety equipment including CE-certified life vests for all occupants — especially children, who should wear vests fitted to their weight class. We recommend carrying a throw line or a small tow float when paddling with non-swimmers, and checking the forecast: inflatables are comfortable in light chop but family boats are best on calm days.

## Matching the kit to the family

For a family of two adults and a child, a common setup is one Kayak Tandem plus one single SUP for the adult who wants a solo paddle. For bigger gatherings, the AirDeck 360 with the Life Vest Classic line covers the group with a single boat. All boards in the family range include the pump, paddle and repair kit, so the only extra purchase is vests sized to the family.

Inflatable construction is the deciding factor for family buyers: no roof rack, no dry-dock storage, and a boat that survives the season in a garage or shed.
