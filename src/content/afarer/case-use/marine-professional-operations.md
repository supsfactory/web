Professional marine operations including law enforcement patrol, search and rescue, and commercial transport rely on afarer's RIB range. The RIB 330 provides coastal transport capability while the RIB 450 Patrol is engineered for extreme conditions with a self-bailing deck, reinforced deep-V hull, and 60 HP engine capacity.

## Where professional RIBs are used

Professional operators use rigid-hulled inflatable boats (RIBs) where reliability and rapid deployment matter more than luxury: police marine units, coastguard and rescue teams, patrol and security operations, and commercial transport between vessels and shore. The rugged inflatable collar provides reserve flotation and shields the working hull from contact with docks, hulls and debris.

## The RIB 330 for coastal transport

The RIB 330 is the workhorse for transport and patrol duties in coastal and harbour waters. Its compact hull is easy to trailer and to launch from a slip, while still carrying crew and cargo for typical patrol runs. For operators requiring a rescue platform, the 330 pairs with the professional life vest range and a small inventory of throw and towing gear.

## The RIB 450 Patrol for extreme conditions

The RIB 450 Patrol is engineered for open water and harsh sea states. Its self-bailing deck clears water automatically, the reinforced deep-V hull parts heavy chop without slamming, and the 60 HP engine capacity delivers the speed and power patrol work demands. The reinforced rub strake protects the boat during high-speed handling alongside docks and other vessels.

## Professional safety equipment

All professional operations require SOLAS-compliant safety equipment including the Life Vest Pro with automatic hydrostatic inflation. For crew who may be unconscious in the water, choose an inflatable vest with an integrated lifting collar; for routine VHF work, pair with the appropriate PFD class. Fire and rescue teams additionally configure their RIB with water-jet, pump and first-response packages.

## Acquiring professional equipment

Professional operators buy through our OEM and government-fleet channels, where we configure RIBs, outboard mounts, electronics and safety kit as a single vessel package, then support service and parts over the life of the hull.
