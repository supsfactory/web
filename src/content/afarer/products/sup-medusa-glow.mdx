## Deep-Sea Dreamscape: Dancing in Harmony with Agile Marine Life

The Medusa Glow Series is inspired by the enchanting, tranquil, and microscopic ecosystems of tropical shallow waters. This inflatable SUP board brings the beauty of the reef to every paddle session.

### Design Philosophy

The nose canvas showcases vibrant dreamy jellyfish, five-pointed sea stars, and well-structured colorful coral reefs against a translucent mint green background —like a flowing underwater tropical rainforest. The EVA pad embeds solid white sea star and seashell die-cut blocks into a deep teal deck for a tactile connection to the beach.

### Craftsmanship

- **Multi-Color EVA Block Piecing**: Sea stars, seashells, and branding are precision die-cut and puzzle-assembled —never printed, so they never fade or peel
- **Anti-Delamination Engineering**: D-ring bungee areas masked for raw PVC-to-PVC bonding
- **Handle Structural Support**: Neoprene webbing anchored directly to PVC hull substrate, not EVA seams

### Performance

- Golden ratio all-around hull with widened midsection
- 2+1 fin setup for ultimate equilibrium between tracking and maneuverability
- Exceptional lateral stability for yoga, pet paddling, and flatwater cruising

### Product Features

- **Drop-Stitch PVC Core**: Military-grade construction rated to 15 PSI for rigid stability
- **32-Inch Wide Deck**: Golden-ratio all-around hull for exceptional lateral support
- **2+1 Fin Configuration**: Dual side fins plus detachable center fin for ultimate equilibrium
- **Bravo Inflation Valve**: High-flow valve for quick inflation and deflation
- **Complete Package**: Includes 3-piece adjustable paddle, dual-action pump with gauge, coiled leash, travel backpack, and repair kit

### Application Environments

- **Tropical Coastal Waters**: Warm lagoons, coral reef-protected bays, and island shorelines
- **Lakes and Ponds**: Calm flatwater for yoga and meditation paddling
- **Beach Resorts**: The mint green and marine palette complements tropical destinations
- **Wellness Retreats**: Wide stable platform for paddle yoga and breathwork sessions

### Who It Is For

- Paddlers who love vibrant marine-inspired aesthetics
- Yoga practitioners seeking a beautiful, stable SUP platform
- Recreational paddlers and social content creators
- Beach travelers who want their board to reflect their destination
