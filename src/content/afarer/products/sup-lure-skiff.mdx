## A Pontoon on the Deck

The Utility Lure Skiff is a rare breed of SUP: a 120 cm-wide, stubby platform built like a pontoon. It is the board anglers choose when stability outranks speed.

### The Signature Cutout

- **Central Cutout Compartment**: A massive square cutout in the deck center — nearly unheard of on conventional SUPs
- **Underwater Viewing Window**: Pairs with a transparent high-definition glass or acrylic window to watch water flow, structures and fish movement beneath you
- **Angling Convenience**: Room to retrofit a livewell, store landing nets, and retrieve lines while seated without rubbing the board edge

### Ultra-Wide Platform

- **120 cm Beam**: Standard all-around boards measure 80–84 cm; this board widens to 120 cm for unmatched lateral stability
- **Stubby Pontoon Outline**: A 350 cm length and swallowtail stern with a dual-thruster setup keep the wide hull maneuverable
- **400 kg Payload**: Reinforced drop-stitch construction carries coolers, rod holders, batteries and even an external electric trolling motor

### Application Scenarios

- **Lure Fishing & Sight Angling**: Observing fish and structure directly beneath the board
- **Seated Casting & Fighting**: Stable angling in a seated position with heavy tackle
- **Motor-Assisted Expeditions**: Pre-configured mounting space for power propulsion systems
