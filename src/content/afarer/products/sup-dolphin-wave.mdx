## Returning to the Pure Freedom of the Ocean

The Dolphin Wave Series is grounded in classic marine aesthetics, masterfully interweaving deep ocean tones with tranquil hues. This inflatable SUP board brings the spirit of the sea to every paddle stroke.

### Design Philosophy

A pod of dolphins leaps gracefully from crashing white foam at the nose, infusing the board with dynamic vitality and forward momentum. The mid-to-rear section features modern geometric color-blocking that transitions into elegant hand-drawn wave linework at the tail.

### Craftsmanship

- **High-Precision EVA Color-Block Splicing**: CNC puzzle-cut and physical splicing process —colors are inherent to the EVA material, never printed
- **360 Marine Aesthetics**: Screen-printed continuous rail waves extend the deck motif around the sides
- **PANTONE TPG Color Matching**: Precise digital benchmarks ensure true-color rendering across production runs
- **150% Flexible UV Ink**: Elongation rate prevents cracking during inflation and storage

### Performance

- All-around hull with widened midsection for lateral stability
- 2+1 fin configuration for balanced tracking
- High-stretch elasticity protects artwork at 15 PSI

### Product Features

- **Drop-Stitch PVC Core**: Military-grade construction rated to 15 PSI for exceptional rigidity
- **32-Inch Wide Deck**: All-around hull with widened midsection for superior lateral stability
- **2+1 Fin Configuration**: Dual side fins plus detachable center fin for straight tracking
- **Bravo Inflation Valve**: High-flow valve for quick inflation and deflation
- **Complete Package**: Includes 3-piece adjustable paddle, dual-action pump with gauge, coiled leash, travel backpack, and repair kit

### Application Environments

- **Lakes and Reservoirs**: Glide across glassy flatwater with effortless tracking
- **Slow Rivers**: Gentle current navigation with responsive maneuverability
- **Coastal Waters**: Sheltered bays and inlets with mild wave conditions
- **Marine Wildlife Hotspots**: The dolphin motif celebrates ocean encounters

### Who It Is For

- Paddlers who love marine wildlife and ocean themes
- Recreational paddlers seeking a distinctive all-around SUP
- Flatwater cruisers and leisure paddlers
- Nature enthusiasts who want their gear to reflect their passion for the sea
