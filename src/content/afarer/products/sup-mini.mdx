## One Board, Every Shoreline Discipline

The Mini SUP Series delivers maximum utility in a compact footprint. Don't let the size fool you — robust buoyancy and a playful shape make it an equally exciting wave-riding tool for adults and a fun floating platform for four-legged companions.

### Core Design

- **Multi-Purpose Hybrid Geometry**: A short, wide, highly stable profile that adapts fluidly to stand-up paddling, prone bodyboarding and wave riding
- **Optimized Volume-to-Length Ratio**: Maximum buoyancy and stability within a compact footprint, supporting a wide range of riders
- **Ultra-Portable Footprint**: Deflates and rolls up into a small package — the quickest board in the lineup to inflate and store

### Key Features

- **Dual-Age Versatility**: A non-intimidating, maneuverable platform for children, rigid enough for adult bodyboarding or rescue use
- **Pet-Friendly Deck**: Heavy-duty, puncture-resistant construction that stands up to dog claws and paws
- **Dynamic Wave Adaptation**: A responsive rocker and rail profile that catches small ocean waves and river breaks standing or prone

### Application Scenarios

- **Youth Paddling & Surf Progression**: Balance, paddle and first-wave training for kids and teens
- **Adult Bodyboarding & Shore Surf**: High-buoyancy bodyboarding in shorebreaks and small surf
- **Pet-Friendly Floating Play**: A safe, balanced day-on-the-water platform for dogs
