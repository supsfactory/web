## Built for the Whole Crew

The Giant SUP is a large-format inflatable engineered for group paddling. Unlike standard boards, an 8-inch-thick hull provides the extreme volume required to float multiple adults while staying rigid under heavy, concentrated loads.

### Core Design

- **8-Inch Thickness Logic**: The extra volume floats multiple riders and prevents bending under concentrated load
- **Swallow or Squared Tail**: Maximum lateral stability when several paddlers shift weight simultaneously
- **Multi-Handle Configuration**: 8–12 heavy-duty neoprene grab handles down both rails plus nose and tail handles for team carrying, launching and re-boarding

### Key Features

- **4+1 Multi-Fin System**: Multiple fins keep the wide board tracking straight and cut lateral drift from current and wind
- **Double Cargo Bungee Systems**: 4-point and 6-point bungee nets at nose and tail for dry bags, coolers and safety gear
- **Safety Leash Points**: Heavy-duty stainless steel D-rings at the tail corners so multiple riders can leash up without tangling

### Application Scenarios

- **Corporate Team Building**: Group events that put everyone on the water together
- **Commercial Rentals & Club Excursions**: High-capacity boards for rental fleets
- **Family & Multi-Kid Lake Parties**: A stable platform for large groups
- **Team Whitewater Rafting**: Heavy-duty double-layer PVC builds for moving water
