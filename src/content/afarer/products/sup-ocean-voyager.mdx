## Harmonizing with Marine Life, Navigating with Steady Grace

The Ocean Voyager Series is a profound tribute to the vibrant yet tranquil world beneath the waves. This inflatable SUP board channels the steady, graceful journey of the sea turtle.

### Design Philosophy

Named for the sea turtle —natures ultimate ocean wanderer —this design bridges sophisticated modern vector aesthetics with high-end manufacturing. The nose features a highly detailed geometric sea turtle surrounded by organic kelp and coral motifs. The traction pad embeds a bold white turtle silhouette into deep teal EVA.

### Craftsmanship

- **Multi-Color EVA Block Piecing**: Complete graphic layout uses die-cut color blocks —never printed, never fades
- **Anti-Delamination Masking**: D-ring attachment areas precisely hollowed out for raw PVC bonding
- **End-to-End Visual Identity**: Roller backpack, pump, leash, and phone pouches all color-matched

### Performance

- All-around hull with widened midsection for maximum lateral stability
- Gently upturned nose rocker for chop-slicing
- Versatile 2+1 fin configuration

### Product Features

- **Drop-Stitch PVC Core**: Military-grade construction rated to 15 PSI for maximum rigidity
- **32-Inch Wide Deck**: All-around hull with upturned nose rocker for chop-slicing
- **2+1 Fin Configuration**: Dual side fins plus detachable center fin for versatile touring
- **Bravo Inflation Valve**: High-flow valve for quick inflation and deflation cycles
- **Complete Package**: Includes 3-piece adjustable paddle, dual-action pump with gauge, coiled leash, travel backpack, and repair kit

### Application Environments

- **Lakes and Reservoirs**: Extended touring and exploration on flatwater
- **Slow Rivers**: Journey downstream with nature-inspired design
- **Calm Coastal Waters**: Sheltered bays and sea turtle habitats
- **Multi-Day Expeditions**: Color-matched travel backpack and accessories make transport effortless

### Who It Is For

- Trend-conscious paddlers and boutique travelers
- Long-distance touring and exploration enthusiasts
- Ocean conservation supporters and marine life advocates
- Paddlers who value coordinated aesthetics from board to accessories
