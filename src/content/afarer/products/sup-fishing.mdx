## A Fishing Boat That Packs Into a Bag

This board stays a proud member of the SUP family, but with a highly specialized niche: an inflatable fishing and expedition platform. It keeps the stand-up paddling and portable inflation of a traditional SUP while integrating the utility of a fishing boat through widened side chambers and tailored mounts.

### Stability by Design

- **Double-Sided Air Chambers**: Robust inflatable side tubes deliver exceptional secondary stability — no more wobbling when standing to fish, reeling in a catch or crossing choppy water
- **Kayak-Like Hull**: A pointed nose and tail with subtle rocker keeps deck width maximum while slicing through waves with clean tracking

### Fishing-First Hardware

- **Rod Holder Mounts**: Two professional-grade mounts ahead of the traction pad secure rod holders, fish finders or other essential gear
- **On-Deck Fish Ruler**: A 10–80 cm ruler printed down the deck center lets you measure the catch directly on the board
- **Multi-Functional D-Rings**: Metal D-rings across the deck for cargo, fishing coolers, folding camp seats and landing nets

### Application Scenarios

- **Stand-Up Angling**: Casting, reeling and fighting fish with a solid footing
- **Expedition Paddling**: Multi-day trips with cooler and gear secured on deck
- **Rough-Water Stability**: Choppy bays and open water where a standard SUP wobbles
