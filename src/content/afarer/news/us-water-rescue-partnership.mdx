### Equipping the Rescuers

afarer has entered into a partnership with the US Water Rescue Association (USWRA) to supply rescue throw bags and life vests for training programs across 30 chapters nationwide.

The partnership covers the Life Vest Pro automatic-inflation life vests for rescue personnel.

The agreement includes discounted pricing for USWRA chapters, dedicated technical support, and joint development of a rescue-specific training curriculum.
