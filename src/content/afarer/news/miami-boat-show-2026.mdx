### Miami Bound

afarer will exhibit at the 2026 Miami International Boat Show, February 11-15 at the Miami Marine Stadium Park and Basin. This marks afarer's debut at North America's largest boat show.

The afarer booth (B-312) will feature the full product lineup with hands-on demonstrations:

- **SUP Explorer 11** —All-around beginner inflatable SUP
- **SUP Tour 12** —Touring inflatable SUP with displacement hull
- **SUP Pro 14** —Performance touring SUP for advanced paddlers
- **SUP Yoga 10** —Wide stable SUP for yoga and fitness
- **Life Vest Pro** —Automatic-inflation life vest

On-water demos will be available by appointment, allowing attendees to test afarer SUPs on the Venetian Basin. afarer executives will also be available for B2B meetings with potential dealers and fleet operators.

Visit afarer.com/miami-boat-show to schedule a meeting or book an on-water demo.
