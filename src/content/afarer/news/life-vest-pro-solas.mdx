### Built for the High Seas

The Afarer Life Vest Pro is designed for commercial marine use — automatic hydrostatic inflation that deploys only on immersion at 0.5–1.0 meter depth, keeping the vest low-profile during normal wear. It includes a spray hood, waterproof light, lifting harness attachment point, and high-visibility reflective tape.

Regulatory requirements differ by market and operating area — international voyages fall under SOLAS, EU-flagged vessels under MED, and US operations under USCG rules. Certification documents — certificates and test reports — are shared with commercial buyers under NDA on request, so your team can verify compliance for the specific route and flag state you operate under.

The Life Vest Pro is priced at $299 and available immediately. It joins the Life Vest Classic (50N+ CE 12402 recreational vest) in the Afarer safety lineup.

Commercial buyers can purchase through the Afarer website or contact the B2B sales team for fleet pricing.
