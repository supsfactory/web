### The Direct Answer

For a custom inflatable SUP, from approved specifications to samples in hand: **15–20 days for the first sample**, then **30–45 days of production** after the PO and deposit for standard OEM runs. If you also need a brand-new mold (custom shape, not an existing platform), add **15–20 days of tooling development** up front. Sea freight then adds roughly 4–6 weeks to most Western destinations. Realistic end-to-end: **about 2–2.5 months from spec approval to a full container**, and **3–3.5 months if the project needs new tooling**.

### Phase 1: Specification and Engineering (within 1 week)

The clock only starts once your specification is complete — shape, sizes, construction targets, graphics, packaging. A well-run factory returns an engineering review within one business day and a quotation within one business day, flagging manufacturability risks (drop-stitch thickness, seam geometry, print placement) before you commit to anything. This phase is free, and it is where good partners spend time asking questions.

### Phase 2: Prototype Sampling (15–20 days)

For graphic-led customizations on an existing platform, the first sample ships in 15–20 days after artwork and specification approval. For a new mold, the 15–20 day tooling phase comes first — the mold is made, mounted and validated before the first board is welded.

The sample contract matters: the factory should document construction, print colors and pressure-test results against your spec, and the sample should arrive with an approval sheet, not just a board.

### Phase 3: Sample Review and Revisions (3–7 days per round)

Budget 3–7 days per revision round: your review, the factory's correction, re-sampling if needed. Two rounds is typical for graphics and construction tweaks; serious geometry changes may push a project back into re-tooling. This is the phase where tight deadlines usually die — plan for at least one revision round in your schedule.

### Phase 4: Mass Production (30–45 days after PO and deposit)

Production runs 30–45 days from confirmed PO and deposit for standard OEM quantities. In-process QC checkpoints, the pressure hold test and the final inspection happen inside this window, and most factories can share batch photos or a video audit while your boards are on the line.

### Phase 5: Export and Transit (4–6 weeks by sea)

After production, boards are packed, containers loaded and cleared. Sea freight to the EU or US typically takes 4–6 weeks; air freight is available for urgent sample or small-batch replenishment but is rarely economical for containers.

### What Shortens the Timeline

- **Give complete specifications up front** — every revision round costs 3–7 days.
- **Adopt a proven platform** — ODM customization on an existing mold skips the 15–20 day tooling phase entirely.
- **Approve artwork in the right format** — print-ready vector files with color references remove prepress loops.
- **Order samples and production sequentially, not serially** — confirm your sample approval within days, not weeks.

The fastest projects are not the ones where the factory works faster — they are the ones where the specification is complete, the sample is approved quickly and the platform already exists.
